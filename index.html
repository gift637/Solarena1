FAQ

Common questions

Is my actual money at risk when trading? +
No. All trading is done with a virtual $100,000 portfolio using real market prices. Only the entry fee (in SOL or USDC) is real. Your trades are simulated — your prizes are real.
How are prices determined? +
All prices are sourced in real-time from Jupiter Aggregator and Birdeye APIs, pulling live data from Solana DEXes including Raydium, Orca, and Meteora. There is no price manipulation possible.
How fast do winnings get paid out? +
Instantly. When a tournament concludes, smart contracts calculate the prize distribution and execute payouts automatically to all winning wallets via Solana. Average settlement time: 0.3 seconds.
What wallets are supported? +
SolArena supports all major Solana wallets including Phantom, Backpack, Solflare, Glow, and any Wallet Adapter-compatible wallet. Mobile wallets are fully supported.
How does the referral program work? +
Share your unique referral link. Every time someone you referred pays an entry fee, you earn 10% of that fee — indefinitely. Earnings are paid out to your wallet weekly. There's no cap on referral earnings.
Are there any geographic restrictions? +
SolArena is available globally with the exception of jurisdictions where participation in skill-based competitions with prizes is legally restricted. Users are responsible for complying with their local regulations.
